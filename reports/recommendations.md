# Recommendations for the marketing team

Derived only from levers the bank controls. Predictors like age, job and
the euribor rate are excluded: they forecast well but cannot be acted on.

## Contact channel
**Route the campaign through cellular rather than telephone**

- Evidence: 14.7% vs 5.2% conversion (1.31x vs base)
- Confidence: high

## Timing
**Concentrate volume in mar, dec, sep**

- Evidence: 48.1% average conversion vs 11.3% overall
- Confidence: high

## Contact frequency
**Stop after 8 attempts on the same customer**

- Evidence: conversion falls below half the base rate beyond that point; 4.3% of all calls are currently spent there
- Confidence: high

## Targeting
**Prioritise customers who accepted a previous campaign**

- Evidence: 65.1% conversion, 5.8x base rate
- Confidence: high

## Measurement
**Hold out a randomised control group from the next campaign**

- Evidence: every figure here is observational, so it shows association and cannot prove that changing the lever causes the lift
- Confidence: n/a - this is how you get causal evidence
